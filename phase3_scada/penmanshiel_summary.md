# Penmanshiel SCADA 2020 — Phase 3 Analysis Summary

## Dataset
- Turbines analyzed: T01, T02, T04, T05, T06, T07, T11
- Year: 2020 (366 days)
- Turbine: Senvion MM82, 2.05 MW, D=82m, hub=59m
- Total operating records: 237,668

## TI Measurement (Direct, IEC-compliant)
- Method: σ_10min / V_10min (from SCADA 'Wind speed, Std' column)
- Site TI range: 0.081 – 0.214 (5th–95th pct)
- Site TI median: 0.1371
- DEL matrix lower bound (extended): 0.08

The Low-TI extension (Phase 5b) is essential for accurate DEL lookup.
